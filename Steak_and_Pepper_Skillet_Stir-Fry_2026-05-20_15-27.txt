RECIPE: Steak and Pepper Skillet Stir-Fry
Date: May 20, 2026 03:27 PM
========================================

INGREDIENTS USED:
  - NY strip steak
  - bell peppers
  - onions
  - garlic
  - zucchini
  - tomatoes
  - butter

MISSING INGREDIENTS:
  - Soy sauce
  - Spice mix (e.g., fajita seasoning)

STEPS:
  Step 1: Prep all ingredients. Slice the NY strip steak into uniform strips (1-inch wide). Chop the onions, dice the bell peppers, and chop the zucchini. Mince the garlic. Keep the tomatoes whole or quartered. Pat the steak dry and season heavily with salt and pepper.
  Step 2: Heat 1 tablespoon of butter in a large skillet or cast-iron pan over high heat. Once the butter melts and coats the pan, carefully add the steak strips, ensuring they are laid in a single layer and do not overlap. Sear the steak for 2-3 minutes per side until well-browned and cooked to your desired doneness. Remove the steak from the skillet and set it aside on a plate. Wipe out any excess residue, leaving about 1-2 tablespoons of butter in the pan.
  Step 3: Reduce the heat to medium-high. Add the sliced onions and bell peppers to the skillet. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes, tossing occasionally, until the vegetables begin to soften and caramelize slightly, about 70% cooked through.
  Step 4: Add the zucchini and tomatoes to the skillet. Continue to stir-fry for another 3-4 minutes, allowing the zucchini to soften and the tomatoes to release their juices. Sprinkle in the minced garlic and stir-fry for 1 minute more until fragrant, being careful not to burn the garlic.
  Step 5: Return the seared steak to the skillet. Pour any accumulated liquid and pan juices over the steak and vegetables. Toss everything gently until the steak is reheated and coated in the flavorful mixture. Cook for 2-3 minutes to finish incorporating all the flavors. Taste and adjust seasoning (salt/pepper) if needed. Serve immediately.
